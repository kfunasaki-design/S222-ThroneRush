## Overview

This guide provides reference information and suggested strategies
for allied guilds within S222 to coordinate their actions during ThroneRush.

The purpose of this guide is to help guilds understand fortress
levels, coordinate attacks, and avoid unnecessary conflicts
between allied guilds.

## Faq

### I want to teleport, but I cannot.

Please return all troops to your castle before teleporting.

> You cannot teleport while troops are outside your castle, such as when attacking monsters, gathering resources, or defending a stronghold.

### When should I teleport?

Please complete your teleport before the event starts.

## Lv7 Fortress

**Target League:** Gold League

Lv7 is the highest-level fortress and is the most limited
strategic location on the map.

---

## Lv6 Fortress

**Target League:** Silver League

Lv6 Fortresses are limited in number, with only three available
on the map.

---

## Lv5 Fortress

**Target League:** Bronze League

### How to Capture a Lv5 Fortress

Starting from Lv5 Fortresses, the number of Imperial Guards
increases significantly.

Each Lv5 Fortress has **120 Imperial Guards**.

![Example](image/lv5-example.png)

### Example

**1. Assign the attacking guild**

Guild ①, located near the Lv5 Fortress, is designated as the
main attacking guild.

**2. Delay the supporting guild's declaration**

Guild ② acts as the supporting guild.

**3. Divide the Imperial Guards**

The guild that defeats the greater number of Guards gains the
right to occupy the fortress.

---

## Lv1–4 Fortresses

**Target League:** No specific GW League requirement.

Please prioritize fortresses located as close as possible
to your initial deployment area.
